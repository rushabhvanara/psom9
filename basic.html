Theory Quick-Ref
Uncompensated: Power transfer is limited strictly by the transmission line's physical reactance. TCSC: Modifies line parameters by reducing net series reactance (Xeff decreases), shifting the P-δ curve upward. SSSC: Injects a controllable series voltage magnitude independent of line current, pushing active power limits higher. UPFC: Provides simultaneous independent control of active power, reactive power, and bus voltage via series and shunt converters.
